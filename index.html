Why Settle 10 Times When You Can Settle Once?

When friends travel or live together, debts become a web of confusion: A owes B, B owes C, and C owes A. Contri uses graph simplification algorithms to resolve circular debts into the minimum possible direct payments.

The Contri Advantage

✓
Aggregated Net Balances: Contri looks at each member's total spending rather than forcing 20 individual person-to-person repayments.
✓
Zero Circular Confusion: Never send $20 to your roommate while he owes you $25 for groceries. Contri computes the net difference automatically.
✓
100% Transparent Proof: Members can expand any suggested payment to see the exact expenses, dates, and mathematical reasoning behind it.
