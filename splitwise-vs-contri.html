Why Millions are Searching for a Splitwise Alternative

For over a decade, Splitwise was the default choice for splitting dinner bills and vacation costs. However, recent monetization changes have left users frustrated:

  • The Infamous Daily Cap: Free users can only add 3 to 4 expenses every 24 hours. If you go on a weekend road trip, you are blocked from logging breakfast, fuel, tolls, and lunch on the same day.
  • Mandatory Countdown Timers: Attempting to add an expense often triggers a forced 10-second waiting screen encouraging users to upgrade to "Splitwise Pro."
  • Expensive Subscriptions: Splitwise Pro costs up to $39.99 per year or $4.99 per month just to unlock receipt scanning and debt simplification.
  • Intrusive Banner Advertisements: The free app interface is cluttered with full-width banner ads that consume screen space and mobile data.
